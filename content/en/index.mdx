Tratto is a modern email service platform for developers. This documentation provides guides, API references, and examples to help you integrate Tratto into your applications.

## What can you do with Tratto?

- **Send emails** — Transactional, marketing, or bulk emails via our API or SDK
- **Manage templates** — Version-controlled email templates with dynamic content
- **Track events** — Real-time delivery, open, click, bounce, and complaint tracking
- **Automate flows** — Build automated email workflows based on triggers and conditions
- **Analyze metrics** — Understand deliverability and engagement with analytics
