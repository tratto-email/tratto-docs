# IP Warming Guide

**IP warming** is the process of gradually increasing email volume on a new IP to establish reputation.

## Why IP Warming Matters

Mail servers track sender reputation **per IP**. A brand new IP has no reputation, so they:
- Throttle delivery (slow ramp-up)
- Send more to spam folder (higher scrutiny)
- May reject outright (very conservative servers)

Warming proves the IP is legitimate and improves deliverability.

## Tratto's Automatic IP Warming

Tratto manages IP warming automatically if you're on a **shared IP pool**. You don't need to do anything.

**For dedicated IPs:** You manage the warm-up. Here's the process.

## Warming Schedule (Typical)

Over **30 days**, gradually increase daily volume:

| Week | Daily Volume | Total |
|------|---|---|
| Week 1 | 100-500 emails | 500 emails |
| Week 2 | 1,000-5,000 emails | 10,000 emails |
| Week 3 | 5,000-10,000 emails | 50,000 emails |
| Week 4 | 10,000-50,000 emails | 200,000 emails |

The exact schedule depends on your ISP monitoring.

## What You Need to Do

### 1. Start Small

Send to your **best engaged recipients first**:
- Customers who opened past emails
- Internal team (guaranteed opens)
- Beta users with high engagement

Avoid:
- ❌ Cold lists
- ❌ Unknown recipients
- ❌ Unverified addresses

### 2. Maintain List Quality

During warming, **every bounce matters**:
- Verify email addresses before sending
- Remove bounces immediately
- Remove unengaged addresses after 6 months

### 3. Monitor Metrics

Check analytics weekly:

```bash
curl "https://api.tratto.email/v1/analytics/summary?period=7d" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer tratto_live_..."
```

Watch for:
- Delivery rate staying 99%+ (good sign)
- Bounce rate < 2% (good sign)
- Complaint rate < 0.1% (critical)

### 4. Consistent Sending

Send regularly (daily, if possible). ISPs like consistency:

✅ 100 emails/day, every day  
❌ 7,000 emails Monday, then nothing for a week

## Throttling Signs

If mail servers are throttling, you'll see:
- Delivery rate dropping below 95%
- Soft bounces increasing
- Slower send times

**Action:** Slow down volume increase for a few days, then ramp more gradually.

## After Warming

Once your IP has good reputation (week 4+):
- Bounce rate stays < 2%
- Complaint rate stays < 0.1%
- Delivery rate is consistently 99%+

You can increase volume faster, but monitor metrics continuously.
